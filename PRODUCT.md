# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Svelte (user-pinned: "simple lightweight app — use Svelte"). Vite + Svelte 5, static build, no backend.

## Users

Czech-speaking people, mostly arriving on a phone from an Instagram story, a shared link or a friend's message. They are deciding whether they could give two young cats a home. Secondary: friends and family following the rescue story.

## Product Purpose

A one-page story site that finds a new home for two rescued kittens. It tells how they were found, how they travelled from Croatia to Prague, why they can't stay, and how to get in touch. Success = a serious adopter writes an email or an Instagram DM.

## Positioning

Not an adoption listing. The cats narrate their own road trip in first person, so the visitor gets to know them as characters before being asked to adopt them.

## Operating Context

- The cats: siblings, a boy and a girl, about 7–9 months old (autumn 2026).
- Names (chosen after the trip): boy **Velebit** ("Velík"), girl **Poljana** ("Polly").
- Found in the forest of the Velebit mountains, Croatia, at the family's Airbnb apartment: cold nights, hungry, they came to the door. The host believes someone dumped them there. The family were the last guests of the season and the host doesn't live there, so nobody would have looked after them.
- Route: Velebit (HR) → stop around Poljana (SI) → Prague (CZ). More stops (e.g. a vet visit) must be easy to add.
- The rescuing family in Prague has two dogs and a cat allergy, so the kittens can't stay.
- Adoption: ideally together; separately is possible.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: Czech only.
- Mobile-first; must work great on phones.
- Journey stops live in data so a new stop is one entry.
- Contact: email + Instagram (placeholders until the user supplies real ones).

## Brand Commitments

- Narrated by the two cats, first person, warm and a bit cheeky.
- Travel-blog / road-trip framing with a car that follows a path as you scroll (the car is a placeholder card for now).

## Evidence on Hand

- Health (confirmed by user, updated 2026-11-03): vet-checked, dewormed, deflead, ears cleaned. Not vaccinated yet; don't claim any vaccination. Velík (the boy) is neutered (confirmed 2026-11-03). Polly isn't neutered: don't mention her neutering on the site, and don't mention that they aren't vaccinated (user's call, 2026-11-03).
- Money (confirmed by user, 2026-10-30): every vet visit so far is paid by the family, and they want nothing for the cats (no adoption fee or contribution), only a good home.
- No real photos yet: every image is a labelled placeholder the user will replace.
- Contact handles are placeholders. Do not invent chipping, neutering, character traits as facts, or dates.

## Product Principles

1. The cats are the narrators; the humans are supporting characters.
2. Every section moves the visitor closer to "I could take them".
3. Honest about why they need a home and what is known about them.
4. Adding a stop or a photo is a data edit, not a design change.
