Velík a Polly: templates for redrawing by hand
================================================

23 pieces, as large as the site allows, each in two versions:

  svg-vector/      SVG templates + .ase colour palettes, for a vector app
                   (e.g. Affinity Designer or Illustrator for iPad)
  png-procreate/   PNG templates + .swatches colour palettes, for Procreate

Both are at 3x the size the piece has on the site, so drawings stay crisp on any screen.
Files are named by their number on drawings.html; each canvas has exactly the
proportions of the spot the piece fills on the site, so a drawing made on it drops
straight in. You can hand in some pieces as SVG and others as PNG.

  M1-M9   whole places on the map: all of Prague, the farm, a mountain range, a forest,
          hills, a lake, fields, the lookout, the picnic spot
  M10     a cloud    M11  the stop pin (waiting and reached, side by side)    M12  the car
  F1-F5   the night forest, one drawing per layer (sky, far forest with the house,
          middle trees, ground, front trees); the layers move separately when scrolling
  F6, O5  the kittens' bodies, tail and ground shadow (the heads are photos)
  O1      the window: night outside, frame, curtains, plant
  O2      the rest of the living room: fireplace with log, picture, candle, floor, rug,
          sofa, lamp
  O3      the fire    O4  the floating heart

Line weight
  The short black line in each template's top-left corner shows the outline width. Every
  template uses the same scale, so it is one width throughout, in pixels on the canvas
  (also the Procreate brush size): 9 px for the forest, the room and the kittens, 6 px for
  the cloud and the pin, about 5 px for the map places.

In Procreate (png-procreate)
  1. Import the .swatches files once (tap them in Files).
  2. Gallery > Import > pick a PNG. It opens as a canvas of the right size.
  3. Lower the template layer's opacity and draw on new layers above it.
  4. Hide the template and the Background colour layer (keep it transparent), then
     Share > PNG. Keep the number at the start of the name ("M1-praha.png").

In a vector app (svg-vector)
  1. Open the SVG, lock the template and lower its opacity; draw above it.
  2. Delete the template and the corner line before exporting.
  3. Export as SVG with the FULL artboard, not trimmed to the drawing, otherwise it will
     not line up. Keep the number at the start of the name ("M1-praha.svg").

Send the files back in the chat.

Good to know
  - You can change shapes freely; they just have to fit the same canvas.
  - Mountains, forests, hills, lakes and fields appear several times on the map. One
    drawing is used for each of them, scaled to each spot.
  - Prague (M1): the map draws the Vltava underneath. Leave it out and the map's river
    shows through, or draw it in; the template shows where it runs.
  - O2 includes the log; the site lays the log back over the flames (O3).
  - Kept in code, also over your drawings: the depth blur of the far forest (F2) and the
    middle trees (F3), so draw those sharp; the sky's colour gradient, the moon's and the
    window's glow, the fog, the flicker of the house window, the firelight, twinkling stars,
    the kittens' breath and shiver, the chimney smoke, and the place names.
  - Not part of the drawings at all: the route, roads, rivers, borders, grass tufts, the
    green wavy edges and the wallpaper. They are generated by code.
